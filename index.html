Attack on Titan

A story of humanity's struggle against giant humanoid creatures known as Titans. Follow Eren, Mikasa, and Armin as they fight for survival and uncover the mysteries of their world. In this epic tale of courage and sacrifice, witness the relentless battle for freedom and the truth behind the Titans. There is three regerment in the world of Attack on Titan. The first regerment is the Military goverment, The second regerment is the Civil goverment and the third regerment is the Religious goverment. The Military goverment is the most powerful goverment in the world of Attack on Titan. The Civil goverment is the second most powerful goverment in the world of Attack on Titan. The Religious goverment is the least powerful goverment in the world of Attack on Titan. The Military goverment is led by a man named Erwin Smith. The Civil goverment is led by a man named Dot Pixis. The Religious goverment is led by a man named Nile Dawk.

More Details

Attack on Titan is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hajime Isayama. It was serialized in Kodansha's Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine from September 2009 to April 2021, with its chapters collected in 34 tankōbon volumes. The story is set in a world where humanity resides within enormous walled cities to protect themselves from the Titans, gigantic humanoid creatures that devour humans seemingly without reason.
